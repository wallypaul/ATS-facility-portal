## Overall Impression

A disciplined, data-first back-office tool that already practices most of what PRODUCT.md preaches. Visual and interaction craft are not the problem; the gaps are workflow-shaped: money trust (stale quotes), the missing recurring-booking path that the entire dialysis use case implies, and dead ends when things go wrong (lockout, no help surface). One measured accessibility failure undermines the WCAG AA commitment.

## What's Working

1. **One honest status system** — a single StatusChip tone map covers booking/payment/invoice states; amber strictly means attention; label text always accompanies color.
2. **Money typography as trust** — IBM Plex Mono + tabular-nums on every amount/ref/date; right-aligned amounts; amber-when-owing balance. The invoice detail survives a CFO's squint.
3. **Every state genuinely designed** — layout-matched skeletons, teaching empty states, 404-vs-error split, reduced-motion and reduced-transparency fallbacks wired at the theme level.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Light-mode amber chips fail WCAG AA — measured 3.46:1** (white 13px text on `secondary.main` #C77700; AA needs 4.5:1). These are the highest-frequency status chips (Received/Unpaid/Sent/Partial); dark mode passes at 8.62:1. PRODUCT.md commits to AA. *Fix:* darken the light-mode chip fill toward #B25E00 (already in palette as warning.main, 4.67:1) or switch filled chips to tinted-bg + dark amber text. → `/impeccable audit`
2. **[P1] Stale quotes displayed after edits** — quotes cache by leg id and never invalidate when pickup/drop-off/service level change; a coordinator can book believing an outdated price. Violates "trusts what the portal tells them about money." *Fix:* clear or visibly mark a leg's quote stale when its inputs change; recompute the sticky total. → `/impeccable harden`
3. **[P1] No repeat/recurring booking path** — dialysis is same patient, same route, 3×/week; today that's full re-entry plus manual quotes. *Fix (frontend-only):* "Book again" on booking rows/detail that prefills the Book form. → `/impeccable shape`
4. **[P1] No account-recovery path on login** — no reset link, no support contact; target users are not power users. *Fix:* reset link if the API supports it, else a "Locked out? Contact ATS at …" line. → `/impeccable harden`
5. **[P2] Trips table addresses truncate with no recovery** — both address columns clip mid-word at 1440px, no tooltip; near-identical rows make "which trip am I cancelling?" unanswerable from the table. *Fix:* tooltips on address cells or a two-line route column. → `/impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user):** no search/quick-filter on Bookings or Trips (and `disableColumnMenu` removes the grid's own filters — zero filtering on Bookings); one-at-a-time cancels with a dialog each; manual Get-quote per leg; no keyboard accelerators at all.

**Sam (accessibility):** the measured 3.46:1 amber chips (above); icon-only row actions with aria-labels but no visible text/tooltip for low-vision users; trash icon means "cancel", not delete; error toasts auto-hide at 7s without pause-on-focus. Verified good: global focus-visible ring, DataGrid focus outlines, role="alert" toasts, labeled nav, chips never color-only.

**Priya (busy facility coordinator, from PRODUCT.md):** free-text addresses mean a typo books "successfully" against the wrong location; an interruption wipes a half-completed Book form (no draft, no guard); "Passengers" under "one patient per booking" is undefined (escorts?); invoice lines mention booking refs in prose but don't link, and trip ids (101) exist nowhere else in her world.

## Minor Observations

- Invoices list (light): header filler right of "Paid" stays paper-white, breaking the header band — paint the filler too.
- Invoices list lacks a **Balance** column — the one number reconciliation needs.
- Trips passes `isLoading || isFetching` to the grid → skeleton flash on focus refetch; Bookings' `isLoading`-only is correct — align them.
- Ref copy icon is always visible at 0.55 opacity though the comment says hover-reveal; hover/focus-reveal would cut noise.
- Route fade (160ms) + grid fade (220ms) stack into a double fade on every table navigation.
- Pagination chrome shows at 5 rows; hide the footer under one page.
- Heading structure: add h2s (or adjust variants) on /book and booking detail (detector: skipped-heading).
- /book leg cards are Card-inside-Card (detector: nested-cards 5×) — visually quiet but structurally the pattern the design bans; consider flattening leg surfaces to bordered Boxes.
- 12px caption text ("Date range", "Patient") is at the floor; fine for labels, don't let body copy reach it.
- Sticky confirm bar re-implements the hairline that `glassTokens.hairline` already provides.

## Questions to Consider

1. Is "Book a trip" the right primary object, or is the real entity a *standing schedule* (patient × route × recurrence) with trips as instances? The biggest efficiency hole disappears if the IA matches the domain.
2. Who is the glass for? On aging facility PCs, `blur(22px)` is pure GPU cost and the reduced-transparency fallback looks nearly identical — should solid be the default and glass the enhancement?
3. Why does the client hold price state at all? A single server-priced "Review & confirm" step would delete the stale-quote bug class and give the money moment the ceremony it deserves.
